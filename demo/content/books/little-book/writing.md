# Writing Content

Chapters are plain markdown files. Headings inside a chapter are
demoted one level so the chapter title becomes the `<h1>` — that is
why the sections below start at `##` even though the source says
`#`.

## Everything is markdown

Text, lists, tables, images: whatever a post supports, a chapter
supports too.

## Sections nest

The two subsections that follow live under this chapter in the
sidebar. If you can tell them apart from top-level chapters at a
glance, the theme is doing its job.
